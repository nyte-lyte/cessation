Cessation is a WIP and has been for over three years. The project has taken several forms during this time as I have spent much of that time trying to understand the best way to represent and explore this idea. Coming from a traditional analog art background, this has been a long journey learning how to express what has been in my head for years. Working in the mediums I was used to working in did not solve this problem. I always approach art as problem-solving and the different mediums as tools to solve those problems. Discovering blockchain as a medium has been life-changing and has opened doors to explore ideas in new ways, but also to explore things I never thought possible.

Living with a rare and deadly cardiomyopathy can be challenging at times. As an artist, I have longed to be able to explore these ideas and share them visually. Blockchain allows for a dynamic and exciting way to do so.
