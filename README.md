Cessation has been a work in progress for over three years, evolving through multiple iterations as I’ve sought the best way to represent and explore its concept and vision. Coming from a traditional analog art background, I began exploring this idea working in familiar mediums that didn’t quite solve the problems I wanted to explore. I’ve always viewed art as a form of problem-solving, with each medium serving as a unique set of tools.

Discovering blockchain as a medium was transformative, opening up new ways to explore ideas. Living with a rare and potentially life-threatening cardiomyopathy presents its own challenges, and as an artist, I’ve wanted to visualize and explore these experiences through the data generated/gathered from my body. Blockchain offers a dynamic, interactive way to do so.
